Title: Alien Swarm Incursion

1. Players will face alien swarms in the incursion starting at 20:00 on October 9. Later incursions rotate through Corona Systems, Alien Lifeforms and the Ink Tide Gang in that order.
2. Acid Bursters close in, burst and self-destruct; they also attack when destroyed at close range. Corrosion reduces the armor and hull resistances of the player's fleet. Brood Workers repair their allies.
3. Hiveback and Broodmother Behemoths rely on Jawclaw Swarms for most of their firepower and replace destroyed swarm units. A living Broodmother also revives Brood Workers and gradually increases the speed of its fleet.
4. Players can salvage ordinary and rare alien incursion wrecks. Rare wrecks can yield Bio Carapace Plates, Bio Damage Control Chambers and Acid Sprayers. Players who destroy the Broodmother receive an Alien Flagship Black Box, which can be used to manufacture ship plugs.
