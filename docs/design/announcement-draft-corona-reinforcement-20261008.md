## 中文正文

- 玩家挑战光环中枢时，中枢每60秒召唤一艘满血叠光级；满编时不召唤，中枢被击毁后停止增援。
- 聚焦阵列的射程加成对光环全队生效，包括新召唤的叠光级；120秒后达到最高加成，中枢被击毁后失效。
- 垂暮级的护盾抗性在闪现进入冷却1秒后生效，持续至本次冷却结束。
- 敌舰详情补齐叠光装置、聚焦阵列与叠光支援信标的效果说明。

## 英文正文

- When players challenge the Corona Nexus, it summons one Corona Overlay at full health every 60 seconds if the formation has room. Reinforcements stop when the Nexus is destroyed.
- The Focus Array grants range to the entire Corona fleet, including newly summoned Overlays. The bonus reaches its maximum after 120 seconds and ends when the Nexus is destroyed.
- Corona Dusk shield resistance activates once blink has been on cooldown for one second and lasts until that cooldown ends.
- Enemy details now include the effects of the Corona Overlay Drive, Focus Array, and Overlay Support Beacon.
