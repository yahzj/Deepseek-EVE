Title: Wreck Recovery and Equipment Buyback Adjustments

1. When players salvage an intact hull, equipment is drawn from civilian, MK1 and the wreck's themed equipment pools instead of always granting the same themed item.
2. The standard equipment pools for rare Corona, Ink Tide and alien incursion wrecks, and rare high-security Gravekeeper wrecks, have been expanded. Players can still obtain equipment exclusive to the corresponding faction.
3. Buyback prices for faction-exclusive and Signal Space-exclusive equipment have been reduced, alongside selected high-value standard equipment. Equipment attributes, purchase prices and black-market prices remain unchanged.
4. Exclusive equipment listed for sale counts as owned until sold, so pending listings no longer make it a priority replacement. Existing items, credits and player listing prices remain unchanged.
